# BRP Field Book

Stormwater (NPDES) Business Registration Program inspection field book. It runs in Safari as a home-screen app and works offline.

Inspections are saved only on the device. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Put it on your iPhone or iPad
1. Open the site address in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open **Field Book** from the home screen once while you have signal. After that it works with no signal.

## Email a report
On the **Review** step, tap **Email Report**, then pick **Mail**. The PDF is attached and the message is written for you. The business email is copied, so paste it in the To line.

## Move inspections between devices
On the home screen, tap **Save Backup File** and save it to Files or email it to yourself. On the other device, tap **Load Backup File**. Loading adds inspections and never deletes any.

## Updating the app
When you change any file, change `VERSION` in `sw.js` so devices download the new copy.
